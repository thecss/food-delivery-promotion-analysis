# 📄 Executive Summary

FoodHub wanted to identify which restaurants and cuisine categories should be prioritized for in-app promotions to improve customer engagement and maximize promotional effectiveness.

Using 1,898 customer orders, restaurant and cuisine performance were analyzed based on order volume, customer ratings, average order value, and ordering patterns.

### Key Findings
* **Top-Performing Restaurants**: Shake Shack (11.5%), The Meatball Shop (7.0%), and Blue Ribbon Sushi (6.3%) were the top-performing restaurants, collectively accounting for 470 orders (24.8% of all orders) while maintaining strong customer ratings.
* **High-Potential Restaurants (Under-Promoted)**: Blue Ribbon Sushi Bar & Grill (4.59★), Five Guys Burgers and Fries (4.56★), and Sushi of Gari Tribeca (4.62★) achieved excellent customer ratings despite receiving only 24–44 orders each.
* **Top-Performing Cuisines**: American, Japanese, and Italian were the three most-ordered cuisines, together accounting for 1,352 orders (71.2% of all orders).
* **High-Rated Cuisines (Growth Opportunity)**: Spanish (4.83★), Thai (4.67★), and Indian (4.54★) received the highest customer ratings despite relatively low demand (12–73 orders).
* **Consistent Ordering Patterns**: Customer behavior remained steady across weekdays and weekends, meaning promotions do not need to vary by day.


### Recommendations
1. Sustain investment in top performers — keep high-performing restaurants and cuisines (Shake Shack, The Meatball Shop, Blue Ribbon Sushi; American, Japanese, Italian) in high-visibility placements.
2. Boost visibility for high-potential options — use targeted promotions to grow demand for highly-rated but under-ordered restaurants and cuisines (e.g., Five Guys, Sushi of Gari Tribeca; Thai, Spanish, Indian).
3. Apply a consistent weekly strategy — since ordering patterns don't vary meaningfully by day, one promotional approach works across the week.
